## 1. What OrigoHOST Is & What It Offers

### Core Identity
OrigoHOST is a full-spectrum developer ecosystem and technology community organization that unites talent, technology infrastructure, and educational opportunities. (`DECIDED`)

### Offerings Breakdown
1. **Developer Education & Upskilling:** Structured training series (e.g., Knowledge Sharing Series 2026), AI workshops, and DevOps bootcamps. (`DECIDED`)
2. **Competitive Engineering & Innovation:** Flagship hackathons (e.g., CyberForge 2026), ideathons, and buildathons. (`DECIDED`)
3. **Ecosystem Infrastructure:** Access to compute, cloud sandbox environments, and deployment resources via Origo Cloud. (`PROPOSED` — availability timeline `TBD`)
4. **Campus Chapter Framework:** Formal operational structure allowing university campuses to launch OrigoHOST student chapters with organizational support. (`DECIDED`)
5. **Open Source & Tooling:** Production repositories, templates, and libraries developed under Origo Dev. (`DECIDED`)
6. **Career & Mentorship Pathways:** Connecting early-stage builders with seasoned industry engineers, speakers, and corporate partners. (`DECIDED`)

- **Ecosystem Growth:** Expand verified developer engagement across Indian academic and professional sectors. (`DECIDED`)

2. **Frictionless Onboarding:** Provide self-evident conversion paths for joining, registering for events, applying for chapters, or contacting organizers. (`DECIDED`)

## 4. Operational Requirements & Guardrails

### Trust Requirements
- **No Fabricated Impact Metrics:** Under no circumstances should placeholder numbers (e.g., "50,000+ members", "99.9% uptime") be displayed unless audited and verified by leadership. (`DECIDED`)
- **Real Team & Speaker Profiles:** Team members, speakers, and advisors must feature verified names, roles, and LinkedIn/GitHub profiles. (`DECIDED`)
- **Auditable Conflict of Interest Policy:** Available under `/about` or `/team` for institutional confidence. (`DECIDED`)

### Partnership & Sponsorship Requirements
- Distinct qualification pathways for Academic Institutions vs. Enterprise Sponsors. (`DECIDED`)
- Sponsorship packages must articulate tangible value (e.g., developer reach, hackathon branding, talent access) without speculative claims. (`DECIDED`)

### Community Requirements
- Code of Conduct clearly articulated in `/terms` and enforced across all digital and physical events. (`DECIDED`)
- Open, accessible participation regardless of institutional pedigree or experience level. (`DECIDED`)
