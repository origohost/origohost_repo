# OrigoHOST — Content Strategy

## 1. Content Mission

> Make OrigoHOST immediately understandable, credible and actionable for every visitor,
> while maintaining factual integrity and the master brand architecture.

---

## 2. Content Goals

| Goal | Success Measure |
|------|----------------|
| Communicate identity clearly | First-time visitor understands OrigoHOST within 30 seconds |
| Make participation obvious | Visitor can identify the right participation pathway without help |
| Enable event/program discovery | Visitor can filter and find a relevant event or program |
| Build institutional credibility | Institution rep can evaluate OrigoHOST as a collaboration partner |
| Enable partner/sponsor inquiry | Potential partner can understand value and submit an inquiry |
| Showcase verified activity | Visitor sees evidence of real events and community activity |

---

## 3. Content Priorities

### P0 — Launch-critical content
- Home page: brand introduction, value proposition, featured events, community CTA
- About page: identity, purpose, vision, mission, ecosystem overview
- Community page: who can participate, how to get involved, pathways
- Events: at least one published upcoming or past event
- Contact: working inquiry form with routing
- Join: published participation pathways
- Legal: Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions
- System pages: 404, 500, Maintenance

### P1 — Post-launch
- Resources: at least one category populated
- Blog: at least one article published
- Team: approved leadership bios
- Partners: confirmed partner records
- FAQ: core categories answered

### P2 — Future
- Gallery populated with event photography
- Blog growing with community content
- Resources growing by technology focus area

---

## 4. Content Principles

1. **Accuracy first** — Never publish unconfirmed facts.
2. **Evidence over hype** — Show real events, real activities, real people.
3. **Specificity** — "Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking Fundamentals webinar" beats "Technology event."
4. **Hierarchy** — Every page has one clear primary message and one primary CTA.
5. **Clarity** — Write for a first-time visitor who has never heard of OrigoHOST.
6. **Freshness** — Stale event dates and outdated statistics damage credibility.
7. **Completeness** — Do not publish empty sections; use meaningful empty states instead.

---

## 5. Key Questions Every Page Must Answer

1. What is OrigoHOST?
2. Why does it exist?
3. Who can participate?
4. What does it do?
5. What events and programs exist?
6. How can I join or contribute?
7. Can my institution collaborate?
8. Can my company partner or sponsor?
9. Where are resources?
10. How can I contact OrigoHOST?

## 7. Content Lifecycle

```
Draft → Review → Approved → Published → Monitored → Updated → Archived
```

- **Draft:** Written but not yet reviewed.
- **Review:** Under review by project lead / content approver.
- **Approved:** Content cleared for publication.
- **Published:** Live on website.
- **Monitored:** Periodically checked for accuracy (especially events, stats, team info).
- **Updated:** Refreshed in place.
- **Archived:** Removed from primary display but retained for reference.

---

## 8. Statistics Policy

Every published statistic must define:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Metric name | "Developers trained" |
| Definition | "Individuals who attended at least one training session" |
| Time period | "As of June 2026" |
| Source | "Internal event registration records" |
| Approved by | "[Name], Project Lead" |

> No statistic may be published without all five fields confirmed internally.

---

## 9. Relationship and Partnership Claims

| Claim | Requirement before publication |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| "Partner" / "Partnership" | Written confirmation from the named organization |
| "Sponsor" | Confirmed sponsorship agreement |
| "Institutional partner" | Signed MOU or equivalent confirmation |
| "Supported by" | Explicit written approval from the named organization |
| "In collaboration with" | Named organization confirmation for the specific initiative |

---

## 10. Content Governance

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Project Lead / Founder | Approve brand claims, partnerships, statistics |
| Content Editor | Draft and update all content |
| Technical Lead | Approve technical accuracy |
| Legal Reviewer | Approve Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions |
| Team Member | Approve own biography and profile |
