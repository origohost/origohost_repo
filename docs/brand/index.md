## Master Brand Statement

> **OrigoHOST is an India-origin technology and community ecosystem connecting infrastructure,
> education, technology, practical experiences and people to help builders learn, create,
> collaborate and become innovators.**

**Tagline:** `Where Builders Become Innovators`

> The tagline must not be replaced or casually substituted with alternatives.

---

## Brand Personality (Proposed — Not Yet Formally Adopted)

Professional · Approachable · Technical · Accessible · Ambitious · Grounded · Curious · Practical · Collaborative · Responsible

---

## What OrigoHOST Is

- A technology community
- A developer and community ecosystem
- An educational ecosystem
- A platform/infrastructure-oriented technology ecosystem
- A practical learning environment
- A connector between people, institutions, technology and opportunities

## What OrigoHOST Is NOT

- Merely a student club
- Merely an event company
- Merely a training institute
- Merely a cloud-hosting company
- Merely a hackathon organizer
- A university, government body or recruitment agency
