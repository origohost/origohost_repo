# Support

## Need Help?

If you need help with the OrigoHOSTs Community platform, please use the following resources:

1. **GitHub Issues**: For bug reports, feature requests, or codebase issues, please [open an issue](https://github.com/origohosts/community/issues).
2. **Community Discord**: Join our Discord server to chat with other contributors and developers.
3. **Email**: Contact us directly at **support@origohosts.com** for business inquiries or critical infrastructure support.

## Security

For security-related issues, please read our [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) guidelines and email **security@origohosts.com**. Do not open a public issue.
